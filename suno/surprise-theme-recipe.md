# Recipe for the surprise theme (2 hours)

The second theme of each day comes two hours before the deadline. Use this plan.

## Time plan

| Minutes | Step |
|---|---|
| 0 to 10 | Fill in the genre sheet below. Write the style prompt. |
| 10 to 25 | Write the lyrics: one hook, one verse, one silly rap or spoken part. |
| 25 to 75 | Generate 6 to 10 takes. Listen to all of them to the end. |
| 75 to 95 | Fix the best take with Extend or Replace section. |
| 95 to 105 | Download WAV. Check tempo. Name the file with BPM and key. |
| 105 to 120 | Spare time. Put the file on the USB stick. |

## Genre sheet

Fill in each line, then join the lines with commas. The result is the style prompt.

1. Era and genre: `90s eurodance`, `80s italo disco`, `2000s trance`, ...
2. Tempo: the normal tempo of the genre (see the table below).
3. Key: one key, for example `A minor`.
4. Two or three typical instruments.
5. The drum pattern.
6. The vocal: who sings, and how.
7. One or two mood words.
8. Always add: `DJ-friendly drum intro and outro`.

## Normal tempos

| Genre | BPM |
|---|---|
| Deep house, garage | 118 to 124 |
| Italo disco | 115 to 125 |
| Disco, nu-disco | 110 to 124 |
| Hi-NRG | 125 to 135 |
| Trance | 132 to 140 |
| Eurodance | 128 to 136 |
| Happy hardcore | 165 to 180 |
| Gabber | 160 to 200 |
| Drum and bass | 170 to 176 |
| Hip hop, boom bap | 85 to 95 |
| Reggaeton | 90 to 100 |
| Schlager, Dutch party music | 120 to 140 |
| Techno | 125 to 140 |
| Hardstyle | 150 |

## Lyric ideas that always work at a hacker party

- Love song where the partner is hardware: a badge, a soldering iron, a router.
- Breakup song in error messages: `segfault`, `404`, `connection reset`.
- A rap that spells a word: `S-E-G-F-A-U-L-T`, `W-I-F-I`.
- A chorus with a call and response: `(say it once again)`.
- A shout-out to the party and the people in the tent.

Ask Claude for the lyrics with the theme name. It can write them in five minutes.
