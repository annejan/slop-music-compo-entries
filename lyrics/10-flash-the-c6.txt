Notes:
- The C6 is the Tanmatsu's ESP32-C6 radio coprocessor. Its USB serial port is the one esptool finds, and opening it resets the radio and crashes the badge. Please do not do it.
- 115200 is esptool's baud rate. Read at the wrong baud rate, the boot ROM's log is noise.
- Hold boot, connecting, the dots and the underscores, and hard resetting via RTS pin are what esptool asks for and prints on the way.
- Guru Meditation Error is what an ESP32 prints when a core panics, and the boot loop is what it does next.
- The authority is the polite MC from The Rhythm Of The Badge, the same voice at the same pitch.
- Every sound is made in code, no samples. The glitches are edits of the song's own audio, and the beat grid never moves.
