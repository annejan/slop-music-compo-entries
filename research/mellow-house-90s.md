# 90's mellow house (Saturday theme)

Goal: a track that sounds like a warm, soulful 1990s house record and that the
DJ can mix with the other entries. "Mellow" points to deep house, garage house
and the softer end of Chicago and French house. It does not point to big-room
or tech house.

## Tempo

- Deep house: 110-125 BPM per Wikipedia, 118-125 BPM per Mixgraph.
- House in general: 120-128 BPM (Mixgraph).
- Garage house (New York / New Jersey): 124-130 BPM (Mixgraph).
- The reference tracks below sit mostly at 119-125 BPM.

**Advice for the compo:** ask for **122 BPM** (safe middle) or 120-124 BPM.
Below 118 BPM the DJ must pitch up a lot. Above 126 BPM it stops sounding
mellow. If the organisers publish a target BPM, use that number exactly.

## Keys and chords

- Mostly minor keys. A minor, F minor, C minor and similar "warm" keys are
  common. Do not rely on Suno to hit a named key.
- The core chord colour is the minor 7th and minor 9th. Guides describe 7th and
  9th voicings as the default for deep house; minor 9ths add "shimmer" over a
  plain m7.
- Typical loops are short: 2 or 4 chords, each held for 1 or 2 bars. Example:
  Am9 - Dm9, or Fm9 - Ebmaj7 - Dbmaj7 - Cm7.
- Chords are played as offbeat stabs (piano, organ) or as long pads.

## Drum programming

- Four-on-the-floor kick. Clap or snare on beats 2 and 4.
- Roland TR-909 is the classic house drum machine. TR-808 sounds (claps,
  cowbell, congas) are also common in deep house.
- Offbeat open hi-hat, 16th closed hats, shakers and congas for groove.
- Swing: guides suggest 55-60% swing on the hi-hats for classic deep house.
  50% is straight; 66% is full triplet swing on an MPC. Some looser styles go
  to 70-80%. Treat these as starting points, not rules.
- Drums are not hard or loud. The kick is round and short.

## Signature instruments

| Instrument | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fender Rhodes / electric piano | minor 9th chords | often with phaser or chorus |
| Korg M1 "Organ 2" preset | organ bass and stabs | the bass on Robin S "Show Me Love" |
| Korg M1 piano ("Piano 8") | house piano riff | used on Black Box "Ride On Time" with 909 drums |
| Roland Juno-60 / Juno-106 | warm pads, saw bass | Larry Heard used a Juno-60 for deep basslines |
| Acoustic / gospel piano | garage chords | New Jersey and New York garage |
| Strings (synth or sampled) | pads, swells | Philly and disco heritage |
| Saxophone, flute, vibes | jazzy leads | St Germain style, Balearic style |

## Vocal style

- Soulful, gospel-trained female "diva" lead is the classic sound (CeCe
  Peniston, Robin S, Barbara Tucker, Ultra Nate).
- Male soul vocals also appear (Ten City, Robert Owens on Mr. Fingers tracks).
- Short repeated hooks, call-and-response, ad-libs, vocal chops.
- Spoken word or whispered lines work for the deeper, darker end.
- Lyrics: love, freedom, unity, "the music", the dancefloor, hope.

## Song structure in bars

House is built in 8-bar phrases, usually grouped as 16 or 32 bars. A
DJ-friendly 6 to 7 minute track at 122 BPM is about 200 bars. Typical layout:

| Section | Bars | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | 16-32 | drums only first (kick, hats), then bass. DJ mixes in here. |
| Groove / chords in | 16 | Rhodes or organ chords enter |
| Verse 1 | 16 | vocal enters |
| Chorus / hook | 16 | full arrangement |
| Breakdown | 16 | kick drops out, pads and vocal |
| Build back | 8 | drums return |
| Chorus / hook | 16-32 | full arrangement |
| Outro | 16-32 | strip back to drums only. DJ mixes out here. |

Radio edits of the same tracks are about 3:30 to 4:00 and cut the long intro
and outro. For the compo you want the club version shape.

## Sub-styles

- **Deep house (Chicago / New York / Detroit):** slower, jazzy chords, muted
  bass, pads, soulful or spoken vocals. Larry Heard, Kerri Chandler, Ron
  Trent, Moodymann.
- **Garage house (New York / New Jersey sound):** gospel piano, organ, big
  female vocal, more energy. Born at the Paradise Garage (New York) and Club
  Zanzibar (Newark, New Jersey). Often 124-128 BPM.
- **Chicago house:** rawer drum machines, simple bass, piano riffs, diva
  vocals. Frankie Knuckles, Marshall Jefferson.
- **French house (late 1990s):** filtered disco samples, phaser, pumping
  sound, 110-130 BPM. Stardust, Daft Punk, Motorbass, St Germain (jazzy).
- **Balearic / chill house:** slower and sunnier, guitars, pianos, ambient
  pads. Sueno Latino, The Beloved.
- **Piano house / Italo house:** big M1 piano riff, sampled vocals. Black Box.
  Upbeat, not very mellow, but era-correct.

## Suno style-tag vocabulary

Suno filters artist names, so describe the sound. Do not write "like Kerri
Chandler". Put the most important words first.

**Genre words:** 90s deep house, soulful house, garage house, New Jersey
garage, Chicago house, classic house, jazzy house, French house, Balearic house.

**Tempo and groove:** 122 BPM, four-on-the-floor, shuffled hi-hats, swing
groove, laid-back groove, steady tempo.

**Drums:** 909 drum machine, 808 claps, congas, shakers, crisp hi-hats, round
kick.

**Keys and harmony:** Rhodes electric piano, minor 9th chords, jazzy chords,
organ bass, house organ stabs, house piano stabs, gospel piano, warm analog
pads, string pads.

**Bass:** deep warm bassline, rubbery synth bass, organ bass.

**Vocals:** soulful female vocal, gospel diva vocal, male soul vocal,
call-and-response backing vocals, vocal chops, spoken word.

**Mood and production:** mellow, warm, late-night, uplifting, dreamy, vinyl
warmth, analog, 1990s production, club mix, extended mix.

Synth model names (Korg M1, Juno, TR-909) are not artist names. They usually
pass the filter, but whether Suno reacts to them is unverified. Back them up
with plain words such as "organ bass".
