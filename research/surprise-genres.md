# Likely surprise themes

Each day gets a second theme, announced 2 hours before the deadline. This
file gives one compact block per likely genre, so an entrant can paste a
prompt and start generating within minutes.

Every Suno prompt below is one line, under 200 characters, and includes a BPM
and "DJ-friendly drum intro and outro". Suno treats the BPM as a hint, not a
lock (see `suno-tips.md`). Check the result in DJ software before you submit.

## BPM overview

"Typical" ranges come from the sources listed at the bottom. Where sources
disagree, the table shows the wider span. "Prompt BPM" is the value used in
the prompt below: a safe middle that DJs can mix.

## Italo disco

- **BPM:** about 110-130. Prompt: 120.
- **Traits:** Italian-produced 1980s synth pop-disco. Drum machines,
  arpeggiated octave synth bass, vocoder, English sung with an accent. Themes:
  love, robots, space.
- **References:** Gazebo "I Like Chopin" (1983); Ryan Paris "Dolce Vita"
  (1983); Baltimora "Tarzan Boy" (1985).
- **Prompt:**
  `80s Italo disco, 120 BPM, accented male vocal, vocoder, arpeggiated octave synth bass, drum machine, dreamy synth melody, DJ-friendly drum intro and outro`

## Hi-NRG

- **BPM:** 120-140. Prompt: 128.
- **Traits:** fast disco descendant. Sequenced 16th-note octave-jumping bass
  that often replaces the hi-hat. Big emotional vocal, gay club heritage.
- **References:** Donna Summer "I Feel Love" (1977); Dead or Alive "You Spin
  Me Round (Like a Record)" (1985); Bananarama "Venus" (1986).
- **Prompt:**
  `80s Hi-NRG, 128 BPM, sequenced octave-jumping bassline, powerful dramatic vocal, synth stabs, handclaps, four-on-the-floor, DJ-friendly drum intro and outro`

## Disco

- **BPM:** 110-130. Mixgraph measures a median near 122 (half of tracks
  between 119 and 124). Prompt: 120.
- **Traits:** four-on-the-floor kick, open hi-hat on the offbeat, broken-octave
  bass, strings and horns, soaring vocals.
- **References:** Bee Gees "Stayin' Alive" (1977); Chic "Le Freak" (1978);
  Sister Sledge "We Are Family" (1979).
- **Prompt:**
  `70s disco, 120 BPM, four-on-the-floor, open hi-hats, funky octave bass, string section, horn stabs, soulful female vocal, DJ-friendly drum intro and outro`

## French house

- **BPM:** 110-130. Prompt: 124.
- **Traits:** filtered loops of late-1970s / early-1980s disco, phaser and
  filter sweeps, pumping compressed sound, often a short repeated vocal hook.
- **References:** Daft Punk "Around the World" (1997); Stardust "Music Sounds
  Better With You" (1998, approx. 124 BPM); Modjo "Lady (Hear Me Tonight)"
  (2000).
- **Prompt:**
  `late 90s French house, 124 BPM, filtered disco loop, phaser sweeps, pumping compression, funky bass, short vocal hook, DJ-friendly drum intro and outro`

## Acid house

- **BPM:** 118-128 (Mixgraph, measured median 126); 120-130 in other guides.
  Prompt: 124.
- **Traits:** squelchy Roland TB-303 bassline with high resonance, slides and
  accents. 808/909 drums. Sparse, hypnotic, few or no vocals.
- **References:** Sleezy D "I've Lost Control" (1986); Phuture "Acid Tracks"
  (1987); Baby Ford "Oochy Koochy" (1988).
- **Prompt:**
  `Chicago acid house, 124 BPM, squelchy 303 acid bassline with resonance sweeps, 909 drums, hypnotic, minimal spoken vocal, DJ-friendly drum intro and outro`

## Big beat

- **BPM:** 100-140. Prompt: 120.
- **Traits:** heavy, distorted, compressed breakbeats. 303 lines and distorted
  synth bass. Samples of 1960s-70s funk, soul and rock. Shouted vocal samples.
- **References:** The Chemical Brothers "Setting Sun" (1997); The Crystal
  Method "Comin' Back" (1998); Fatboy Slim "Praise You" (1999).
- **Prompt:**
  `late 90s big beat, 120 BPM, heavy distorted breakbeat, funk guitar sample, acid synth bass, shouted vocal samples, rave energy, DJ-friendly drum intro and outro`

## Techno

- **BPM:** 120-150 (Wikipedia); 130-140 (Mixgraph). Prompt: 132.
- **Traits:** repetitive 4/4 for continuous DJ sets. Rhythm over melody. 808
  and 909 drums, synth stabs, sequencers. Mostly instrumental.
- **References:** Derrick May "Strings of Life" (1987); Inner City "Big Fun"
  (1988); Jeff Mills "The Bells" (1997).
- **Prompt:**
  `90s Detroit techno, 132 BPM, driving 909 kick, rolling percussion, hypnotic synth stabs, string chords, instrumental, DJ-friendly drum intro and outro`

## Trance

- **BPM:** 120-150; production guides say 125-150; Mixgraph says 130-145.
  Prompt: 138.
- **Traits:** builds to one or two peaks, long breakdown with no beat, supersaw
  leads (Roland JP-8000), arpeggios, sparse intro and outro for DJs.
- **References:** Humate "Love Stimulation" (Paul van Dyk remix, 1993); Quench
  "Dreams" (1993); Robert Miles "Children" (1996).
- **Prompt:**
  `late 90s uplifting trance, 138 BPM, supersaw lead, rolling bassline, long breakdown with arpeggios, euphoric female vocal, DJ-friendly drum intro and outro`

## Eurobeat

- **BPM:** 145-165, 150-160 most common (genre guides and fan sources;
  approx.). Tempos rose through the late 1990s. Prompt: 155.
- **Traits:** Italian production for the Japanese market (Super Eurobeat, Para
  Para, Initial D). Layered synth riff ("sabi"), electric guitar from the
  mid-1990s, dramatic English vocals, fast octave bass.
- **References:** Max Coveri "Running in the 90s" (approx. 1998); Dave Rodgers
  "Deja Vu" (approx. 1999); Niko "Speedway" (2000).
- **Prompt:**
  `90s Eurobeat, 155 BPM, fast octave bass, layered synth brass riff, electric guitar, dramatic male vocal, high-energy racing mood, DJ-friendly drum intro and outro`

## Hardstyle

- **BPM:** about 140 early (2000s), 150-160 later (Wikipedia); Mixgraph says
  148-155. Prompt: 150.
- **Traits:** overdriven, distorted kick that also carries the bass pitch;
  offbeat "reverse bass"; anthemic lead and breakdown; strongly Dutch scene.
- **References:** artists to study (track years unverified): Headhunterz,
  Coone, Brennan Heart. Headhunterz "Dragonborn" (approx. 2012).
- **Prompt:**
  `euphoric hardstyle, 150 BPM, distorted pitched kick, reverse bass, anthemic supersaw lead, epic breakdown, male vocal hook, DJ-friendly drum intro and outro`

## Happy hardcore

- **BPM:** 160-175 (Mixgraph); UK tracks approx. 170 (Wikipedia). Prompt: 170.
- **Traits:** four-on-the-floor kick plus breakbeats, piano rolls, uplifting
  pitched-up female vocals, stabs. Later tracks use a distorted 909 kick.
  Dutch "happy gabber" sits a bit faster.
- **References:** DJ Paul Elstak "Luv U More" (1994); DJ Paul Elstak "Rainbow
  in the Sky" (1995); Force & Styles "Heart of Gold" (mid-1990s).
- **Prompt:**
  `90s happy hardcore, 170 BPM, pitched-up female vocal, piano rolls, rave stabs, breakbeats under four-on-the-floor kick, euphoric, DJ-friendly drum intro and outro`

## Gabber

- **BPM:** about 140-190 (Wikipedia); 160-200 (Mixgraph). Prompt: 180.
- **Traits:** Rotterdam, early 1990s. Heavy distorted kick drum, hoover synths,
  shouted MC samples, aggressive and fast. Very Dutch.
- **References:** Rotterdam Termination Source "Poing" (1992); DJ Chosen Few
  "Chosen Anthem (Against Racism)" (1993); Party Animals "Have You Ever Been
  Mellow" (1995).
- **Prompt:**
  `90s Rotterdam gabber, 180 BPM, heavily distorted kick drum, hoover synth, shouted MC samples, hard rave stabs, aggressive, DJ-friendly drum intro and outro`

## Drum and bass

- **BPM:** 160-180, mid-170s typical (Wikipedia); 170-180 (Mixgraph).
  Prompt: 174.
- **Traits:** fast syncopated breakbeats (the Amen break), deep sub-bass,
  rolling energy. Liquid (soulful) and jungle (ragga, chopped breaks) variants.
- **References:** Goldie "Inner City Life" (1994); Roni Size / Reprazent
  "Brown Paper Bag" (1997); DJ Fresh "Hot Right Now" (2012).
- **Prompt:**
  `90s liquid drum and bass, 174 BPM, chopped amen breakbeat, deep rolling sub bass, jazzy Rhodes chords, soulful female vocal, DJ-friendly drum intro and outro`

## Synthwave

- **BPM:** 80-118 common, upbeat tracks 128-140 (Wikipedia); 80-110
  (Mixgraph). Prompt: 118 to sit next to house.
- **Traits:** 1980s film-score and video-game nostalgia, analog synths, gated
  reverb drums, arpeggiated bass, mostly instrumental.
- **References:** Kavinsky "Nightcall" (2011); College and Electric Youth "A
  Real Hero" (2011); The Weeknd "Blinding Lights" (2019, single).
- **Prompt:**
  `80s synthwave, 118 BPM, gated reverb drums, pulsing arpeggiated analog bass, lush retro pads, neon night drive mood, airy female vocal, DJ-friendly drum intro and outro`

## Chiptune

- **BPM:** no genre tempo. Use the tempo of the dance style you wrap it in.
  Prompt: 130 (chip-eurodance).
- **Traits:** sound-chip timbres (C64 SID, NES, Game Boy): square and pulse
  waves, fast arpeggios to fake chords, noise-channel drums, 8-bit leads.
- **References:** Rob Hubbard "Commando" (C64, 1985); Yuzo Koshiro "Streets of
  Rage 2" soundtrack (1992); Anamanaguchi "Endless Fantasy" (2013).
- **Prompt:**
  `chiptune dance, 130 BPM, 8-bit square wave lead, fast arpeggios, C64 SID bass, punchy four-on-the-floor kick, retro game music, DJ-friendly drum intro and outro`

## Nederlandstalig / schlager / carnaval

- **BPM:** no reliable genre source found. Measured examples: Wolter Kroes
  "Viva Hollandia" (2008) 128 BPM; Snollebollekes "Links Rechts" 150 BPM;
  German apres-ski hit "Mama Laudaaa" (Specktakel) 140 BPM; K3 "Feest" 127 BPM.
  Treat 125-150 as approx. Prompt: 140.
- **Traits (carnavalskraker):** brass (trumpets, tuba), driving bass, simple
  repeated chorus you can sing after one listen, cheeky wordplay, topical
  jokes, little text. Modern versions add house or "feest" beats. Limburg
  stays more traditional (brass band / "blaaskapel"); elsewhere more party
  house.
- **References:** Wolter Kroes "Viva Hollandia" (2008); Snollebollekes "Links
  Rechts" (year unverified); CV De Weggooiers "Nog un Likkie" (2024 "Kies je
  Kraker" winner).
- **Prompt:**
  `Dutch carnaval party schlager, 140 BPM, Dutch lyrics, oompah brass band, tuba bass, four-on-the-floor party beat, sing-along chorus, accordion, DJ-friendly drum intro and outro`
- **Note:** have Claude write the Dutch lyrics. Suno sings Dutch, but
  pronunciation varies (unverified per model version). Keep the chorus short
  and repetitive.
